Precision starts
with honest boundaries.

  • CSV and XLSX two-column label/value tables
  • DOCX tables and label/value paragraphs
  • Text-based PDF label/value lines
  • TXT and Markdown label/value lines
  • Scanned PDFs need OCR; OCR is not included
  • Complex layouts and footnotes may be missed
  • Engineering impact and compliance are not inferred
  • Extraction completeness must be reviewed
