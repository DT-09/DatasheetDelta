04 / WHAT A FINDING CARRIES

Not just a redline.
A trail to inspect.

Each register entry is designed to retain the before and after values, source references and comparison explanation. That gives a reviewer a starting point they can challenge—not a verdict they must trust.

  • Original extracted values
  • Source row, sheet, page or paragraph where available
  • Unit and comparison explanation
  • Warnings and unresolved extraction limits
  • Portable JSON and CSV outputs

05 / SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

Precision includes
knowing the limits.

DatasheetDelta is a document evidence tool. It is not a substitute for technical interpretation or formal change control.

01Scanned documents

Scanned PDFs need OCR. OCR is not included.

02Complex layouts

Multi-column pages, merged tables, graphics, images and footnotes may be missed.

03Repeated labels

Duplicate normalized labels generate warnings. Inspect every source occurrence.

04Engineering judgment

No automated materiality, safety, compliance, contract or suitability decision.

05Operational controls

No authentication, persistent audit log, sign-off or approval workflow.
