Precision starts
with honest boundaries.

  • CSV and XLSX label/value tables
  • DOCX tables and label/value paragraphs
  • Text-based PDF label/value lines
  • TXT and Markdown label/value lines
  • Scanned PDFs require OCR; OCR is not included
  • Complex layouts, drawings, footnotes and merged structures may be missed
  • Repeated labels are preserved and marked ambiguous
  • Engineering impact, safety and compliance are not inferred
