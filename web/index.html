02 / METHOD

From source document
to reviewable evidence.

01

Extract

Read supported label/value pairs. Retain filename and row, sheet, table, paragraph, page or line location.

02

Normalize

Convert a limited set of compatible units while keeping original source values visible.

03

Compare

Classify added, removed, changed, unchanged, unit-mismatched and ambiguous parameters.

04

Review

Verify each result against the source documents. A qualified engineer determines materiality and approval.

03 / SCOPE AND LIMITS

Precision starts
with honest boundaries.

  • CSV and XLSX label/value rows and horizontal key/value pairs
  • DOCX tables and label/value paragraphs
  • Text-based PDF label/value lines
  • TXT and Markdown label/value lines
  • Scanned PDFs are not OCR-processed
  • Complex layouts, footnotes and nested tables may be missed
  • Duplicate parameter labels are flagged as ambiguous
  • Engineering impact and compliance are not inferred
  • Extraction completeness must be checked by a human
