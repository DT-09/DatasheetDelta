PRODUCT BOUNDARIES / READ FIRST

Trust depends on
what we don't claim.

DatasheetDelta is an evidence-gathering assistant. It does not replace engineering judgment or certify a product.

  • Scanned PDFs require OCR; OCR is not included.
  • Complex multi-column layouts, merged tables, graphics, images and footnotes may be missed.
  • Duplicate labels are surfaced as warnings; inspect every occurrence.
  • No automated materiality, safety, compliance or supplier-contract decision.
  • No sign-off, approval workflow, customer integration or persistent document archive.
